Incident Command

Build a Fire Station or Command HQ for ground units, or an Airport for aircraft.

Air tanker: purchase, then tap the target area. Water tenders refill at roadside hydrants, water depots or riverbanks. Water units refill and return to base when fires are out. Damaged units return to a Fire Station for repairs. Ground units favor roads. Off-road travel is slower unless a dozer has cleared the ground. Automatic units prioritize fires threatening homes. Idle dozers cut perimeters around active fires. Helicopters carry four drops per standard tank. Use Suppress to target an air tanker pass. It flies across the wind and lays retardant along its flight path.

Fire behavior: Fires persist until suppressed, even inside containment lines. Wind can carry embers over roads and firebreaks, including two-tile dozer lines at 30 mph or more. Grass spreads quickly; dense forest burns hotter and needs more water. Engines defend nearby structures; crews can cut firebreaks.

Controls: Click a unit to select it, or left-click and drag a box to select multiple units. Shift-click adds or removes units. Orders affect all selected units. Left-click the map to issue the selected order (Suppress by default). Right-click to cut line. On touch, use the Orders buttons and tap the map. 1 Move 2 Suppress 3 Firebreak 4 Refill Space Pause.
